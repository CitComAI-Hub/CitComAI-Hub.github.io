    The CitCom data space

    Where CitCom partners share data and AI services under their own rules. Operated by VRAIN-UPV for every partner, built on the FIWARE Data Space Connector, and open to any organisation that joins.

    Shared services · operated by VRAIN-UPV for all of CitCom

    Onboarding portal An organisation asks to join Trust anchor The list of trusted organisations, checked on every access Marketplace Providers publish offers, consumers buy them
    1Both join
    2Publishes an offer
    3Buys it
    Provider · example Ajuntament de València Its connector serves the city data it offers Connector
    4 Reads the data, connector to connector
    Consumer · example VRAIN-UPV Its AI service, the MIAIS, reads the data it bought Connector
    1. 1Join. Both organisations register in the onboarding portal. Once approved, they are in the trust anchor and their credentials are trusted.
    2. 2Publish. The Ajuntament offers a dataset in the marketplace, for example its waste containers.
    3. 3Buy. VRAIN-UPV buys the offer. The purchase sets up the contract in the Ajuntament's connector.
    4. 4Read. The MIAIS of VRAIN-UPV reads the data from the Ajuntament's connector. The data never goes through the shared services.
