The Valencia TEF site belongs to the South super-node (CONNECT). It supports AI innovation in pollution, noise, waste and water management, mobility, urban development and tourism.

## Who is who

| Organisation | Role in the TEF |
|---|---|
| **Ajuntament de València** (City Council) | Owns the city data, through the **VLCi** urban platform |
| **València Innovation Capital** | Innovation hubs: Las Naves and La Harinera, the Urban Sandbox for AI |
| **VRAIN-UPV** (Universitat Politècnica de València) | AI research and development; operates the [CitCom.ai Data Space](../../data_spaces/index.md) |

## Services for companies

- **Dissemination of AI activities**: workshops, webinars and presentations that showcase the innovations tested in Valencia.
- **Development and testing of AI algorithms**: the universities of the consortium help startups and research centres develop and validate their algorithms, with attention to data privacy and optimisation.
- **City data**: real-time data from the VLCi platform, offered through the CitCom.ai Data Space. An independent instance of VLCi was also set up for CitCom partners to experiment in a controlled environment.
- **Regulatory support**: the Urban Sandbox for AI helps test in real conditions and check compliance; S2Grupo provides cybersecurity services.

## In the data space

The Ajuntament de València was one of the first participants of the CitCom.ai Data Space. It serves its NGSI-v2 platform through [Lepus](../../toolbox/lepus.md) and offers four datasets in the [marketplace](../../marketplace/index.md). How it was done is told step by step in the worked example [Valencia: a city platform in the data space](../../valencia/index.md).
